💡 How to Achieve Maximum Measurement Accuracy ▼
  1. Gentle Fingertip Contact: Rest the fleshy pad of your index finger lightly over the rear camera lens and LED flashlight. Do not press hard—firm pressure blanches blood capillaries and restricts microvascular blood flow.
  2. Stable Hand Position: Keep your hand relaxed and resting on a table or surface to minimize motion artifacts.
  3. Sensor Warmup: Allow 3–5 seconds for the optical baseline DC filter to calibrate and stabilize.
  4. Flashlight Control: On mobile browsers supporting torch constraints (Chrome/Android), the flash turns on automatically. On iOS Safari, ensure ambient lighting is sufficient or toggle your flashlight from Control Center if prompted.
